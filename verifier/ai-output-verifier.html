Why verification matters

        

          Measured performance is high for producing work with AI and low for checking it.
          The loss sits in that verification gap.
        

        

          

            66%
            of employees rely on AI output without verifying it first.
            KPMG × University of Melbourne, global trust study
          

          

            8.5%
            of users consistently verify AI outputs. Nearly 30% say they almost never spot AI falsehoods.
            Shanghai Jiao Tong University / CAS survey, 2025 (n=811)
          

          

            84.5% → 60–70%
            Consultants’ accuracy fell by that much when an AI answered confidently — and wrong — on a task beyond its limits.
            Dell’Acqua et al., Harvard Business School, 2023
          

          

            37%
            of the time AI saves is lost again to fixing its output — about 4 hours back for every 10 gained.
            Workday × Hanover Research, Nov 2025 (n=3,200)
          

          

            $186
            per employee, per month — the estimated cost of “workslop”: polished AI output with nothing underneath.
            BetterUp Labs × Stanford, Harvard Business Review, Sept 2025
          

          

            69%
            of UK financial-services staff say they’d feel more confident if every AI output were captured and checked.
            Smarsh UK financial-services survey, June 2025
          

        

        

          Figures are as reported by the cited studies (research snapshot, 17 Oct 2026). Several
          were established from secondary reporting rather than the primary papers, and some come
          from vendor-run surveys — the exact numbers are directional; the pattern is consistent:
          verification is the ranked #1 inefficiency of the three this suite addresses.
